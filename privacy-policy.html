FEM Wallet Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FEM Wallet help website at help.femchain.online handles information. It describes the website currently provided: informational pages, without account registration, contact forms, analytics tools, or advertising features.

The FEM Wallet project is responsible for this website. The project contact identified to us is Leonine Marvel, CEO and Founder. No separate registered company or business address has been provided for this policy.

Scope: This notice covers the help website only. It does not describe data collected by a FEM mobile app, browser extension, wallet, or blockchain service. Those products need a separate, accurate notice based on their actual code, SDKs, and service providers before this URL is used as their app-store privacy policy.

1. Information the Website Handles

Website requests and server logs

When your browser requests a page, Vercel, the website hosting and delivery provider, may process technical request information such as your IP address, requested URL, request time, browser or device user-agent, referring page, response status, and security events. These details are generated by normal website delivery and may appear in Vercel infrastructure logs.

Support email

The website does not have a contact form. If you email femwallet@gmail.com, FEM receives the sender address, message, any information you choose to include, and any attachments. The mailbox is a Gmail address, so Google processes the message to provide email services.

Cookies, analytics, and browser storage

The current website source does not set or read cookies, use browser local or session storage, or include an analytics or advertising script. Vercel may use infrastructure technologies needed to deliver or protect its service; consult Vercel’s privacy information for its processing. We will update this notice before adding non-essential cookies, analytics, or similar tools.

Do not send passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, or other wallet secrets by email. This help website does not request or need them.

2. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Vercel request and security information is used to deliver pages, protect the site from abuse, and diagnose availability or technical errors. Where applicable, the basis is legitimate interests in operating and securing the website, or compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Support email content is used to understand and respond to your request and maintain relevant follow-up records. Where applicable, the basis is taking steps at your request, legitimate interests in providing support, or consent when you choose to provide optional information.
  • Information may be used or retained where needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims or comply with law.

Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 or another data-protection law applies, we rely only on a lawful basis available under that law and respect its requirements.

3. Service Providers and Other Disclosures

  • Vercel: hosts and delivers the website and may process request and security metadata for that purpose.
  • Google (Gmail): processes messages sent to the support mailbox so FEM can receive and respond to them.
  • Authorities: information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or the website.

The current website does not use a separate analytics, advertising, ticketing, or marketing-email provider. FEM does not sell personal information.

Vercel and Google may process information in countries other than yours, including where their infrastructure or service providers operate. Where required, applicable transfer safeguards are the responsibility of the relevant provider and FEM. See the providers’ privacy terms for details: Vercel Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Retention

Support emails are kept while needed to respond and manage follow-up. They are then deleted when no longer reasonably needed, unless a longer period is required to meet legal obligations or handle a security issue or dispute.

Website request logs are subject to Vercel’s logging and retention controls, which may depend on the account plan and settings. FEM uses them only for delivery, security, and troubleshooting purposes and retains or accesses them no longer than reasonably needed for those purposes, subject to provider retention and legal requirements. Contact us to ask about the current applicable retention period.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; receive a portable copy; or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, email femwallet@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject and describe what you need. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. If the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 applies, you may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

6. Security and Children

The website is served over HTTPS by Vercel. FEM and its providers use safeguards appropriate to their roles, but internet transmission and storage cannot be guaranteed completely secure. Do not email wallet credentials or secrets.

This informational website is not directed to children, and FEM does not knowingly seek to collect children’s personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information may contact us to request its deletion.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this notice when website practices or legal requirements change. The latest version and effective date will be posted on this page. For a material change, we will add a prominent notice on the website where reasonably practicable.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the FEM Wallet project through femwallet@gmail.com. Project contact: Leonine Marvel, CEO and Founder.
