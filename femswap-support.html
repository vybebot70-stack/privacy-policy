FEM Swap · Support

How can we help?

Contact the FEM project about FEM Swap.

Contact support

Email femwallet@gmail.com and include “FEM Swap” in the subject. Describe what happened and, when relevant, include the network, app/browser version, and public transaction hash. Do not include personal information that is not needed to investigate the issue.

Keep your wallet safe

Support will never ask for your recovery phrase, private key, wallet password, one-time code, or remote access to your device. Anyone asking for these secrets is not legitimate support. Never approve a transaction just because someone claiming to be support tells you to.

Transaction and wallet limitations

Support cannot access your wallet, recover its secret keys, cancel a pending transaction, or reverse a confirmed blockchain transaction. For wallet connection issues, use the official help resources for your wallet provider. Verify that you are using the official FEM Swap address before connecting.

Privacy requests

For a question about information handled by FEM Swap, email femwallet@gmail.com with “FEM Swap Privacy Request” in the subject. See the FEM Swap Privacy Policy for more information.
