FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the FEM Wallet help website and support.

What is this website for?

This is FEM Wallet’s informational help website. It provides support contact details, safety guidance, and legal notices. It does not itself access wallets or process blockchain transactions.

Does this Privacy Policy cover the FEM wallet app?

No. The Privacy Policy on this website describes this help website only. App users should be given a separate policy that accurately reflects the app’s data collection, SDKs, permissions, and service providers.

What does non-custodial mean for FEM?

FEM is being built as a non-custodial wallet: users, rather than FEM, are intended to control their wallet keys and authorize transactions. Non-custodial does not by itself mean the app collects no technical data; consult the app’s own privacy notice for its actual practices.

How do I contact FEM support?

Email femwallet@gmail.com. For an app issue, include the app version, device and operating system, and relevant non-sensitive error details.

What should I never send to support?

Never send a recovery phrase, private key, wallet password, one-time code, or remote-access credentials. See the security guidance.

Can FEM support recover my wallet or reverse a transaction?

FEM is being built as a non-custodial wallet, so support should not need your recovery phrase or private key and cannot use this help website to access your wallet. Recovery options depend on the wallet’s actual setup. Confirmed blockchain transactions are generally difficult or impossible to reverse; check the relevant network and app guidance.

How can I report a suspicious message or security issue?

Email femwallet@gmail.com with a description and relevant public links or transaction details. Never include wallet secrets. FEM has not published a dedicated disclosure program or response-time guarantee.

What information does this website collect?

Hosting providers may process technical request and security information, and the support mailbox receives information you choose to email. The site currently has no contact form or analytics script. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where can I read the rules for using this site?

Read the Terms of Service. These terms cover the help website, not a separate wallet app or third-party service.
