About FEM Wallet Help

Official help, safety, and policy information.

A clear place to find help

This website brings together FEM Wallet help information, safety guidance, support contact details, and legal notices. It is an informational website; it does not itself access wallets, hold assets, or process blockchain transactions.

FEM is being built as a non-custodial wallet

Non-custodial means the wallet is designed so users, rather than FEM, control their wallet keys and authorize transactions. It does not mean the app necessarily collects no technical data. See the separate app privacy notice when available for the app’s actual data practices.

Who is responsible

The FEM Wallet project operates this help website. The project contact identified to us is Leonine Marvel, CEO and Founder. No separate registered company name or business address has been provided here.
